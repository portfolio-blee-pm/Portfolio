Situation

A national rollout with no playbook

The organization had a mandate to launch a gig-aggregation platform nationally — but no program infrastructure to execute it. No repeatable launch model, no cross-functional governance, no SLA framework, and an aggressive corporate timeline that had already been deemed unrealistic by internal stakeholders.

Three markets were live. The ask was 25+. The window was less than a year.

"We had the product. We had the ambition. What we didn't have was a program architecture that could scale a market launch every two to three weeks without breaking — across vendor dependencies, regulatory requirements, and distributed ops teams."

No standardized launch playbook across markets
8-week market cycle with no compression model
Risk and dependency management done ad hoc
No executive visibility layer across the portfolio
Approach

Architecture before acceleration

The instinct in high-pressure rollouts is to move fast and fix things later. The decision here was the opposite — invest two weeks in infrastructure before touching velocity. That discipline is what made the compression possible.

⬡
Launch Architecture
Designed a repeatable market-launch framework — standardized phases, decision gates, vendor onboarding sequences, and go/no-go criteria that could run in parallel across multiple markets simultaneously.
◈
Governance at Scale
Built a full SLA, risk, and dependency governance model — with escalation paths, accountability matrices, and cadence structures that kept distributed teams aligned without creating coordination overhead.
◎
Cycle Compression
Systematically identified the critical path constraints in the 8-week cycle. Restructured sequencing, parallelized workstreams, and pre-staged dependencies to cut the launch cycle to 4 weeks.
Execution

How 3 became 25+

The rollout wasn't a single sprint — it was a compounding system. Each market launch refined the playbook. Each cycle got tighter. By month six, the team was launching markets in parallel with a reliability rate that had never been seen at this scale.

Mo 1–2
Infrastructure Sprint
Built launch framework, governance model, SLA structure, and risk register. Staffed cross-functional pod. Established baseline cycle time and identified compression levers.
Mo 3–4
Pilot Markets & Cycle Compression
Launched markets 4–8 using new framework. Compressed cycle from 8 to 6 weeks. Identified remaining critical path bottlenecks in vendor and regulatory dependencies.
Mo 5–7
Parallel Launch Cadence
Achieved 4-week cycle. Began running 2–3 market launches simultaneously. Zero P0/P1 incidents across the portfolio. Roadmap moved from 18 months to 9.
Mo 8–9
National Scale Achieved
25+ markets live. Full SLA compliance. Program governance handed to steady-state ops. Documentation and playbooks transferred for ongoing replication.
