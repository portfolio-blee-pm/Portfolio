Program Overview

The Challenge

The organization needed to scale a gig-aggregation platform from 3 pilot markets to a national footprint — fast. The corporate roadmap projected 18+ months. Leadership needed it done in under a year. There was no existing program infrastructure, no governance framework, and no repeatable launch playbook.

Operational teams were executing launches reactively — each market was a custom effort with no standardized onboarding, SLA structure, or dependency tracking. Risk was invisible until it became an incident.

The constraint: Launch velocity had to increase while simultaneously reducing risk exposure. Every new market added systemic complexity — billing integrations, driver onboarding pipelines, API dependencies, and multi-stakeholder coordination across ops, engineering, and finance.

Strategic Approach

Rather than treating each market as a standalone project, the program was architected as a repeatable operational system. Build once, launch everywhere. The governance model, launch playbook, and operational infrastructure came first — before scaling, not during.

Three parallel workstreams ran simultaneously: infrastructure standardization, launch execution, and governance buildout. Weekly Go/No-Go reviews with cross-functional leads ensured nothing fell through the cracks.
