Principles

Four things I believe about running programs

Decide who decides
Most program delays are really unresolved decisions. I write decision rights down early, so escalations have somewhere to go.
Contracts before code
Agree on interfaces, schemas, and definitions before building on them. It's the cheapest point to find a disagreement.
Gates, not heroics
Reliable launches come from clear exit criteria and owned risks, not from late nights before go-live.
Automate assembly, keep judgment
Tools and AI should collect and draft. People decide and stay accountable for what gets published.

Scaling

Matched to the team's size

At a 20-person startup, intake is a short, ranked list and one decision-maker. At enterprise scale, it becomes a portfolio review with Finance in the room. The mechanism stays the same and its weight changes. Bringing enterprise-weight process into a small team slows it down just as much as having no process at all.
