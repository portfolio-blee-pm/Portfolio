02

The Framework.
How I Build & Sustain PMOs.

No inherited playbook. Every governance layer was designed from scratch — rooted in PMI PMP and PgMP standards, extended with hybrid Agile@Scale delivery, AI-augmented validation, and value-stream thinking. Built for high-velocity, multi-market operations and battle-tested across 25+ simultaneous launches.

Go/No-Go Gate System
Multi-criteria launch gates — operational readiness, data integrity, regulatory compliance, dependency clearance. AI-augmented predictive scoring with mandatory human final authority at every gate. Criteria iteratively tightened after each launch based on retrospective data. Full audit trail: timestamp, decision rationale, owner, and outcome logged for every gate. I personally review and sign off every package — no exceptions, no shortcuts.
Modular Playbook Architecture
Version-controlled, traceable, audit-ready from pilot onward. Modular structure: onboarding, routing, dispatch, escalation, market exit. AI used post-update to diff versions, flag inconsistencies, and suggest minimal fixes. Every launch feeds precise updates before the next cycle. I personally approve major revisions to maintain governance integrity — playbooks are living, value-centric documents, never static compliance artifacts.
SLA & KPI Governance Layer
Real-time predictive monitoring via monday.com — custom formulas, threshold alerts, anomaly flagging, and value realization dashboards tracking ROI, customer outcomes, and benefits vs. baseline. I personally review and recalibrate thresholds to ensure SLAs are exceeded as standard. Anomalies surface before they become incidents — sustaining 99%+ uptime across programs.
Risk Register & Scenario Engine
Probability/impact scoring + early warning indicators per initiative and market. AI-simulated edge cases — 300% volume surge, 40% no-show rate, cross-state regulatory mismatch. Pre-launch mitigation playbooks (prevent/detect/respond) written before go-live. I maintain the master register, personally gate risk decisions, and update mitigations based on emerging data throughout the program lifecycle.
Cross-Functional Orchestration
Stakeholder management elevated to program scale: RACI matrices, decision logs, structured escalation paths, and weekly steering cadences. Hybrid/distributed team alignment across engineering, ops, logistics, and executive stakeholders simultaneously. I personally chair steering reviews, resolve interdependencies, and remove blockers — ensuring concurrent workstreams never create single points of failure.
Continuous Iteration Engine
Lean Six Sigma–influenced retrospectives formalize lessons from every cycle into structured gate, playbook, KPI, and validation refinements. The compounding loop is what makes market 25 materially stronger than market 1. I personally drive this improvement engine — leading retros, approving changes, and ensuring the PMO evolves faster than the challenges it governs.

06

How I Run
Programs.

The principles that governed 25+ market launches, three active client programs, and every delivery decision in between — built from operational experience, not theory.

Gates Are Non-Negotiable
I enforce project-level controls — scope, risk, quality — at program scale. No launch proceeds without full gate clearance, even under pressure. I personally review every gate package, balancing AI recommendations with real-world context and stakeholder input. This discipline delivered zero critical failures across 25+ launches and remains my non-negotiable standard.
Playbooks Are Living & Value-Centric
Playbooks evolve after every cycle — I lead retrospectives to capture what broke, what slowed velocity, and what was unclear, then feed precise updates forward. Focus stays on outcomes: benefits realization, ROI, and delivery value rather than rigid process adherence. I personally approve significant revisions to ensure governance integrity is never compromised.
Human Authority Remains Paramount
AI excels at scoring, simulation, synthesis, and gap detection — but I make every final Go/No-Go, escalation, and refinement decision. Emotional intelligence, stakeholder trust, and nuanced judgment ensure ethical, defensible outcomes in complex, high-stakes environments. Human-in-the-loop authority is core to my PMO philosophy — not optional.
Audit Trails Create Resilience
Every gate outcome, playbook update, KPI recalibration, incident escalation, and retrospective insight is logged with timestamp, rationale, owner attribution, and version history. I enforce this standard to enable full traceability, defensibility during audits, and seamless knowledge transfer — turning the PMO into a long-term strategic asset, not a single point of knowledge.
SLAs Are the Floor — We Aim to Exceed
SLA targets are minimum acceptable thresholds — my goal is always superior performance. I personally review real-time dashboards and recalibrate thresholds to ensure predictive monitoring catches anomalies before they become breaches. 99%+ uptime and 22% downtime reduction weren't end-state targets; they were the result of governing to them every single day.
Iteration Compounds Long-Term Capability
Lean Six Sigma–influenced retrospectives plus data analytics create a compounding loop: each cycle refines gates, playbooks, KPIs, and validation layers. The PMO at market 25 is materially stronger than at market 1 — I personally drive this improvement engine, ensuring the system evolves faster than the challenges it governs.
