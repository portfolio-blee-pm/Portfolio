Program Overview

The Challenge

Scaling to 25+ markets generated an enormous manual operations burden. Market readiness assessments were done by hand. Routing logic decisions required analyst hours per market. Executive reporting was a weekly manual effort pulling from a dozen sources. The team was growing, but the overhead was growing faster.

The question wasn't whether to use AI — it was where AI would have the highest operational leverage and how to implement it responsibly within a program context where accuracy was non-negotiable.

The approach: AI was deployed as a decision-support and validation layer — not a replacement for human judgment. Every workflow was designed with a human review step for high-stakes outputs. The goal was speed and consistency, not autonomy.

Strategic Approach

Three high-leverage workflows were identified where AI could provide the most immediate operational value: market readiness scoring, routing logic validation, and executive reporting synthesis. Each was designed, tested, and iterated before full deployment.

Claude was used for executive reporting synthesis and decision support validation. GPT-4 was used for market readiness scoring support and routing logic validation — processing structured inputs against defined criteria.
