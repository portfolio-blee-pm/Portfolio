Program Overview

The Challenge

As the National Aggregation Program scaled to 25+ markets, operational visibility collapsed. Teams were managing launches through fragmented spreadsheets, disconnected project tools, and ad-hoc reporting. Leadership had no single source of truth — no real-time view of what was live, what was at risk, or what needed decisions.

Executive stakeholders were receiving weekly status emails that were outdated before they landed. Cross-functional dependencies were invisible until they became blockers. The program was scaling faster than its reporting infrastructure could keep up.

The gap: Operational teams had the data — it was scattered across monday.com boards, JIRA tickets, Salesforce records, and manual trackers. The Command Hub was built to unify it into a single operational intelligence layer that leadership could act from in real time.

Strategic Approach

The Command Hub was designed as a decision-support system, not just a dashboard. The architecture was built around three layers — operational status, risk surfacing, and executive action.

Existing platforms were integrated and structured: monday.com became the operational spine, JIRA fed engineering dependency tracking, Salesforce connected market revenue data, and Claude synthesized the executive narrative layer.
