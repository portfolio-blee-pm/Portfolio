Situation

Scale creates overhead. AI eliminates it.

At 25+ markets, the volume of operational decisions — routing, readiness scoring, reporting synthesis — had outpaced what human teams could handle with acceptable latency. The work wasn't complex. It was repetitive, rule-bound, and time-consuming.

The opportunity wasn't to replace judgment. It was to automate everything that didn't require it — and free the team to focus on decisions that actually did.

"The bottleneck wasn't capacity. It was that the team was spending 40% of their time on work that followed a pattern — routing logic, status synthesis, readiness checks. That's not strategy. That's overhead."

Claude (Anthropic) GPT-4 Agentic Workflows Predictive Routing Automated Synthesis
Approach

Three automation domains. One ops layer.

Rather than building isolated tools, the approach was to architect a unified AI operations layer — three interlocking domains that together eliminated the pattern-work from the ops cycle entirely.

Domain 1 · Routing Intelligence
Predictive routing logic — AI-driven decision engine that evaluates market conditions, vendor availability, and capacity signals to route work automatically. Eliminated manual triage and reduced routing latency from hours to minutes.
↓
Domain 2 · Reporting Synthesis
Executive reporting automation — LLM-powered synthesis layer that transforms raw operational data into structured executive briefs, status summaries, and portfolio narratives. Replaced 6–8 hours of weekly manual compilation.
↓
Domain 3 · Market Readiness Scoring
Automated readiness assessment — scoring model that evaluates pre-launch market criteria across 30+ variables, surfaces risk flags, and produces go/no-go recommendations — replacing manual checklists and review cycles.
What Made It Hard

The architecture challenge wasn't the AI

⬡
Trust Engineering
Ops teams needed to trust automated outputs enough to act on them. That required building in explainability, confidence scoring, and human-in-the-loop checkpoints at the right friction points — not just everywhere.
◈
Failure Mode Design
Agentic workflows fail differently than manual processes. Failure modes had to be explicitly designed — graceful degradation, escalation triggers, and audit trails that made every automated decision reviewable.
◎
Integration Constraints
The AI layer had to sit on top of existing data infrastructure — not replace it. Designing clean interfaces between legacy operational data and LLM pipelines was the technical constraint that shaped every architecture decision.
